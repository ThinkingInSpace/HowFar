Privacy & credits

Updated October 5, 2026.

Playing GeoRange

GeoRange has no accounts, ads, tracking cookies, or location requests. Guesses and scores are calculated in your browser. Reloading resets your game. Code, city data, and globe imagery load from this website’s host.

Aggregate research results

To study geographic distance knowledge, we count game starts and completions, average completed scores and score ranges, and answers and average percentage errors for each route. Daily and practice totals are kept separately in monthly groups. Route errors are rounded to 0.1 percentage point; errors above 1,000% are counted separately and excluded from averages.

During play, the browser sends a game-start signal, each answered route with its rounded error, and the final score after five answers to our Cloudflare collector. It immediately adds these to aggregate totals. Our application does not save individual submissions, game histories, names, IP addresses, browser details, exact play times, or player identifiers. It does not transmit your raw distance guesses. Aggregates are retained for the study; reports are private, and route comparisons require at least 20 ordinary answers.

You can choose “Don’t include my results” in the game footer before playing or at any time. This stops future submissions and saves only an opt-out preference in this browser’s local storage. “Include future games” resumes collection at the next game. Previously combined totals cannot be linked to you or removed individually. Your choice applies separately in each browser and on each website address. If browser storage is unavailable, collection starts disabled and choices last for the current page. Local previews do not collect results.

Cloudflare processes network connection information to deliver and secure the collector, and GitHub processes connection information to host the game. These providers may retain operational records under their own policies, even though our collector’s request logging and tracing are disabled. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We describe the application’s retained research data as aggregate-only; this does not mean the underlying network requests are anonymous to the hosting providers.

“Copy my score” writes your result to your clipboard only when you press it. Nothing is posted automatically. If copying is unavailable, the game provides a text box for manual copying.

The public site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links on this page open other sites with their own privacy practices.

Data & imagery

Made with Natural Earth. The 243 city names and coordinates match the Natural Earth 5.1.2 populated places dataset. Natural Earth data is public domain.

Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble, distributed with the Three-globe 2.45.2 example. See NASA’s imagery usage guidelines. NASA does not endorse this game.

About the distances

This is a casual geography game. Distances approximate the shortest surface route on a spherical Earth between city coordinates; they are not flight, driving, or navigation instructions. Place names reflect the source dataset and do not express a position on sovereignty or borders. Scores are calculated locally and are not independently verified.

Report problems through the project’s issue tracker. Reports are public; do not include private information.
