🌍 Great Circle Guesser

Five rounds. Fifteen seconds each. Guess the shortest distance over the Earth’s surface, not the length of an actual flight.

How scoring works

Earn up to 200 points per round. A guess 10% away earns 180 points; 50% away earns 100. A miss of 100% or more, or running out of time, earns zero. Points are rounded to the nearest whole number. The timer continues if you switch tabs.
